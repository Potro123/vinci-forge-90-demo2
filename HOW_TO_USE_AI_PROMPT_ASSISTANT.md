# How to Use the AI Prompt Assistant 🎨✨

The AI Prompt Assistant is now **fully functional** and ready to help you create amazing prompts!

## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Open the Assistant
1. Go to the main generation page
2. Look for the button that says **"Show AI Prompt Assistant"**
3. Click it to reveal the assistant panel

### Step 2: Describe Your Idea
In the "Your Idea" text box, describe what you want to create. For example:

**For Images:**
- "Create a new image with a perfect replica of da Vinci Mona Lisa"
- "A futuristic city at sunset with flying cars"
- "A cute cartoon cat wearing a wizard hat"

**For Videos:**
- "A drone flying over a mountain landscape"
- "Time-lapse of a flower blooming"
- "Cinematic shot of a car driving through rain"

**For 3D Models:**
- "A low-poly medieval sword"
- "A realistic coffee mug with steam"
- "A stylized character for a game"

**For CAD:**
- "A mechanical gear with 20 teeth, 50mm diameter"
- "A mounting bracket for industrial equipment"
- "A precision shaft with bearing seats"

### Step 3: Enhance with AI
1. Click the **"✨ Enhance with AI"** button
2. Wait 2-5 seconds while the AI works its magic
3. The enhanced prompt will appear below

### Step 4: Use the Enhanced Prompt
You have two options:

**Option A: Use Directly**
- Click **"Use This Prompt"** to automatically fill the main prompt field
- The assistant will close and your enhanced prompt is ready to generate!

**Option B: Copy & Edit**
- Click the **copy icon** (📋) to copy the enhanced prompt
- Paste it wherever you want
- Edit it further if needed

## Quick Templates

Don't know where to start? Click any of the **Quick Templates** at the bottom of the assistant to get instant inspiration!

## Tips for Best Results

✅ **Be Specific**: The more details you provide, the better the AI can enhance your prompt
✅ **Use Descriptive Words**: Include colors, styles, moods, lighting, etc.
✅ **Mention the Medium**: Specify if you want photorealistic, cartoon, sketch, etc.
✅ **Avoid Sensitive Content**: No violence, weapons, adult content, or hate symbols

## Example Workflow

1. **Your Idea**: "burger"
2. **Click**: ✨ Enhance with AI
3. **AI Enhanced**: "A professional food photography shot of a gourmet burger with melted cheese, fresh lettuce, and sesame seed bun, studio lighting, 4K, highly detailed, appetizing presentation on a wooden board"
4. **Click**: Use This Prompt
5. **Generate**: Create your amazing image!

## Troubleshooting

**"Please sign in to use the AI Prompt Assistant"**
- You need to be logged in to use this feature
- Click the sign-in button and create an account

**"AI service temporarily unavailable"**
- The AI service is experiencing high load
- Wait a moment and try again
- The system automatically tries 3 different AI models for reliability

**No response after clicking**
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page and try again
- Check the browser console for any errors
